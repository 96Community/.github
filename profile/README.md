# 96Community

### Technology for real-world problems.

96Community is a technology community focused on identifying small, meaningful problems people face in everyday life and building practical solutions for them.

We believe good engineering starts with understanding the problem — not choosing the technology first.

> **Don't chase the moon. Fix the pothole in front of you.**

## What we do

* Identify real-world problems
* Understand the people experiencing them
* Design practical solutions
* Build and test software
* Learn from real usage
* Share useful technology with the community

## Our approach

**Problem → Understand → Build → Test → Improve**

We don't build technology simply because it is possible or popular.

We build it because there is a problem worth solving.

## Projects

Our projects are experiments and solutions built around real problems.

Each project documents:

* The problem
* Who experiences it
* The proposed solution
* The technology behind it
* What we learned
* What happens next

## Get involved

Have a small problem that you think technology could help solve?

Open an issue and tell us about it.

Have technical skills and want to help build?

Contributions are welcome.
